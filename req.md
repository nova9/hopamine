Hopamine Virtual Events Website Outline

Colors: White, light and deep blue, black, yellow highlights as needed
Alignment: Left to right alignment

General idea: 
Mods create an event, server members add submissions (presentations, doc with speeches) under the event details. These submissions should be able to be added by anyone and seen by anyone. The goal is to: 
Make all information presented at the events still accessible for non-attendees.
Gauge and track participation.
Inform members of all upcoming and past events.
